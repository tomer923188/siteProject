About Me

I am a Linux enthusiast dedicated to the philosophy of free software and system minimalism. This website serves as a documentation of my journey through different distributions and my transition from bloated desktop environments to efficient, tiling window managers.

My Philosophy

I believe that the user should have total control over their hardware. That is why I moved from Arch to Gentoo—to understand every compilation flag and package that goes into my kernel. For me, computing isn't just about getting things done; it's about knowing how they get done.


My Goal

Through this site, I hope to share my configurations (dots) and help others who are looking to ditch systemd or dive into the world of suckless software like dwm and st.

Hardware & Setup

  • Main Desktop: Gentoo Linux (No Systemd)
  • Laptop: ThinkPad running Artix (OpenRC)
  • Window Manager: dwm (Custom patched in C)
  • Shell: Zsh / Bash
